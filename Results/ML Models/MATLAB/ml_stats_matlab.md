## S3. Agreement of the IMU with the camera

**Foot lift (Bland-Altman).** Dot foot-sensor rise above its stance height vs camera toe-marker rise above its stance height (the sensor sits on the dorsum, the marker on the toe). Bias = mean(Dot - camera); limits of agreement (LoA) = bias +- 1.96 SD; proportional bias = slope of the difference on the mean of the two. **Toe-off time.** Step-4 IMU toe-off (foot-angle minimum) minus the camera toe lift-off; its change per cm of obstacle from `dt ~ h + (1 | participant)`.

**Reading.** The foot sensor and the toe marker measure different points of the foot, so a bias is expected; the limits of agreement give the crossing-to-crossing disagreement a toe-height estimate from the foot sensor would carry (the reason clearance is only partly predictable, `ml_validate_matlab.md` V3). The IMU toe-off event comes after the camera lift-off, much later for the trailing limb, which is why every timing in the haptic analyses uses the camera lift-off.

**Reading.** When the dominant leg leads (so the non-dominant leg trails), the crossings differ significantly in: lead foot lift (Dot) -21.2 mm; lead thigh flexion (Dot) -5.42 deg; lead minimum clearance (camera) -34.4 mm; trail shank heel-up (Dot) -2.55 deg; trail minimum clearance (camera) +46.0 mm.
