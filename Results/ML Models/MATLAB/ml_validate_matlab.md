## V3. What sets the toe clearance, and what the IMU can see of it

Two camera quantities could set it: how HIGH the toe goes (camera toe-marker peak above the obstacle top) and WHERE the foot leaves the floor (take-off distance: toe before the near edge at the limb's own toe-off). The IMU measures the first (foot lift = foot-sensor rise; toe rise = rigid-body toe estimate, provisional) but has no reference to the obstacle position.
