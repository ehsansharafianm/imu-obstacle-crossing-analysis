# Can the walker measure the environment? (MATLAB)

_Generated by `Analysis/ML/crossing_ml_environment.m` on 2026-11-11 09:42, MATLAB 2023b._

260 obstacle crossings (include_ml), 10 participants. Every estimate is for a participant left out of training (LOPO). The camera gives the targets (training and scoring only); the inputs are the Dot IMUs. Inputs per limb and sensor site: thigh / shank = tilt and angular-velocity summaries; foot = foot tilt, angular velocity and acceleration, the foot trajectory (ZHC) summaries, the 13 clearance-shape features, swing and stance time (+ the approach strides for the leading foot); leg length is added to every set. Normalisation: **generic** = minus the person's level-walk median, lengths / leg length; **person** = (x - own median) / own IQR over the person's obstacle crossings (no labels). Models: ridge (lambda 10, z-scored) and random forest (300 trees, min leaf 3). Metrics: mean absolute error (MAE), Pearson r, and r **within cells** (target and estimate minus their participant x obstacle means: does the estimate follow the crossing-to-crossing changes?). Baseline = the training participants' mean. **Calibration** = a few camera-labelled crossings of the new person (1, or 3 = one per height; 20 random draws) shift the estimate by its mean error on them; compared with simply using the mean of those labelled crossings (own mean).
