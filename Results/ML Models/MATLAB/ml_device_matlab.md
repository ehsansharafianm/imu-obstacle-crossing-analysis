**Reading.** Two foot IMUs are enough - they match or beat all six sensors. A device that has seen ~10-15 of the user's crossings (no labels) or one labelled crossing per height reaches ~85-92%, and when it answers only above 0.7 probability it is right ~97% of the time. The learning curve is flat from a few training participants: the remaining errors come from person-to-person differences, which the calibration addresses, not from the training-set size.
