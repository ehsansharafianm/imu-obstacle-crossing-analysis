Each limb has its own model (lead features only / trail features only). **Fusion** = average of the two models' class probabilities (soft vote); features are never mixed. Leave-one-participant-out on 10 participants; 95% CI = participant bootstrap. **Normalization:** *level* = minus the participant's level-walk median, lengths / leg length (as before); *self* = each participant scaled to their own median and IQR over the analysed crossings (no labels used, but it assumes the person's recent crossings span a mix of obstacles, which the design guarantees here - an optimistic setting).
